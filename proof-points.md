| ID | Finding |
|---|---|
| R-10 | 4 of 5 beta customers named roster sync as a benefit they hadn't anticipated |
| R-11 | 3 of 5 saw a cross-department benefit (finance or operations seeing compliance data they didn't have before) |

## G. Claim guardrails

**Say, and attribute correctly**
- Company-wide numbers (94%, 11 hours, 4 days, 100% audit compliance) are averages across customers. Case-study numbers belong to the named customer.
- SyncBridge: "live in under 3 hours with minimal IT support." "No code" is accurate.
- "Runs itself" is figurative. Pair it with the support line: "Your vendors upload and renew their own certificates. You manage the exceptions."

**Tier labels.** Claims about these features should say which plan includes them:

| Feature | Plan |
|---|---|
| RiskMap, configurable reminder sequences, escalation routing, audit-ready PDF export, full compliance history | Professional and Scale |
| SyncBridge, compliance-gated POs, API, SSO | Scale |
| Live in 4 days, guaranteed | Scale |

Portal, reminders at 90/60/30 days, expiry tracking and basic reports are on every plan.

**Don't say**
- "Real-time" sync, "no IT," "no IT ticket" or "fool-proof."
- That TraceDesk beats enterprise suites on breadth. Compete on scope, price and time to value.
- That every competitor lacks a vendor portal. Say instead: "No other tool in the mid-market price range combines a full vendor self-service portal, automated escalation and multi-document risk scoring."
- "Available to existing customers at no cost." SyncBridge is part of the Scale plan; Professional customers can upgrade at any time, prorated.
- Any award, ranking or analyst recognition.

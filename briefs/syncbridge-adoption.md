# Campaign Brief
Campaign name: SyncBridge Adoption
Goal (one measurable thing): Raise SyncBridge adoption among paying customers from 25% to 35% (low-end goal) or 50% (high-end goal) by the end of the campaign. This brief is internal to the team; keep adoption figures out of customer-facing copy. (company-facts.md shows 41 of 68 Scale customers connected.)
Audience (which ICP segment): Existing customers running SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite or Microsoft Dynamics 365. Economic buyers (COO, VP Operations, Director of Procurement, CFO or VP Finance) and technical evaluators (IT & Procurement Systems Manager, IT lead). Two tracks: (1) Scale customers who haven't connected SyncBridge: it's already on their plan, so they only need to turn it on. (2) Professional customers on an ERP: SyncBridge needs an upgrade to Scale, available at any time, prorated.
Offer / key message: Your vendor roster and your ERP tell the same story. New vendors added in your ERP appear in TraceDesk automatically, and compliance status writes back to your ERP vendor records. It's not an IT project: no code, live in under 3 hours with minimal IT support. Hypothesis: adoption is low because customers don't know about it, so this campaign re-introduces it. Why it matters internally: an ERP-connected account is harder to replace (retention). Keep that argument out of customer copy.
Proof points to use: S-07 (under 3 hours, minimal IT support); SyncBridge GA figures (41 customers connected; average setup 2 hr 25 min; median IT involvement 25 minutes; 15–20 minute sync window; ERP is the source of truth); beta findings R-10 to R-13; beta before/after table (BlueLine, Ironclad, Vertex, Cascadia, Midwest Component). Quotes: T-40 to T-48 and T-55 to T-59 from testimonials.md.
Assets needed (e.g. 3 emails, 5 LinkedIn posts, 1 landing page outline): First round: 1 email (email 1 of the drip, Scale track) and 1 LinkedIn post. Full campaign later: 3-email drip, 4 LinkedIn posts, 1 webinar proposal and outline, webinar sign-up page copy (the webinar can appear in one or more emails).
Webinar: Live SyncBridge walkthrough, November 17, 2026, 1pm Eastern. Invite appears in email 1 (P.S.); title, speakers and length to be set in the webinar outline.
Deadline: November 15, 2026
Anything to avoid: Calling SyncBridge free, "at no cost" or included for everyone (it's on the Scale plan only). "Real-time" (say "within 15–20 minutes"). "No IT" or "no IT ticket" (say "minimal IT support"). "Enterprise-grade." Ambiguous phrasing like "scale on Scale." Any adoption percentage in customer copy. The retention or stickiness argument in customer copy.

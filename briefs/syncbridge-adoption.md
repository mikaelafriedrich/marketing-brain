## Goal

### Primary: 75% of Scale customers connected to SyncBridge

- **Baseline:** 41 of 68 Scale customers connected (60%).
- **Target:** 51 of 68 (75%). That's **10 more connections**, from a pool of 27 Scale customers who haven't connected.
- **Measured:** December 18, 2026, about four weeks after the webinar.
- If new Scale customers sign during the campaign, measure against the 75% rate rather than the count of 51.

### Secondary: 5 Scale demos booked by Professional customers

- **Why demos, not upgrades:** Professional customers need to move to Scale for SyncBridge, and Scale requires a demo. A booked demo is the first measurable step, and it's enough to start the conversation.
- **Is 5 reasonable?** Yes, as a floor. 10 would be a stretch.
  - **For it:** demand already exists. In the Q1 2026 NPS survey, 26 of 34 detractors (76%) cited missing ERP integration. 62 of 97 passives (64%) named a specific fixable gap, ERP integration most often. Both surveys came before SyncBridge was generally available.
  - **For it:** Cascadia Foods passed on TraceDesk in late 2024 because their VP of Finance needed SAP visibility. They came back within a week of the SyncBridge announcement and signed within two, without a demo.
  - **Against it:** we don't know how many of the 302 Professional customers run one of the three ERPs. That number decides whether 5 is easy or hard. **Pull it from the CRM before launch.**

### Webinar target: 40 registrations, 20 live attendees

- **Registrations:** 40.
- **Live attendees:** 20. This assumes about half of registrants attend, a common B2B webinar rate. It's an assumption, not TraceDesk data.
- **The number that matters most:** at least **8 of the 27 unconnected Scale accounts** attend live. The primary goal needs 10 more connections, and the webinar is the main push.
- **Count accounts, not just people.** A buyer and an IT lead from the same company are one account. Track the two separately.
- **Revisit after the CRM pull.** If the Professional ERP pool is large, raise registrations to 60.

### Why this matters internally

An account whose compliance status lives in its ERP is harder to replace, so this campaign is also a retention play. **Keep that argument out of customer-facing copy.**

## Audience

### Track 1: Scale customers who haven't connected (27 accounts)

- **What's true for them:** SyncBridge is already on their plan. They only need to turn it on.
- **Hypothesis:** they don't know it's there, or assume it's an IT project.
- **Call to action:** Connect SyncBridge, or reply to their dedicated CSM for help.

### Track 2: Professional customers running an ERP (pool size to be confirmed)

- **What's true for them:** SyncBridge is on the Scale plan. They can upgrade at any time, prorated.
- **Call to action:** Book a demo.

### Who we're writing for

- **Economic buyer:** COO, VP Operations, Director of Procurement; CFO or VP Finance on larger accounts. They frame the value as **risk avoided**.
- **Technical evaluator:** IT & Procurement Systems Manager or IT lead. Their question is **"Is it another silo?"** The answer: the ERP stays the source of truth, and there's no custom integration project.
- **Also reached:** Finance and AP. They see compliance status in the ERP vendor record before approving POs or invoices.

---

## Key message

**Your vendor roster and your ERP tell the same story.**

1. **New vendors added in your ERP appear in TraceDesk automatically.** Lead with this. Roster sync makes it structurally impossible for a vendor to slip through the manual import step.
2. **Compliance status writes back to your ERP vendor records.** Finance sees it without a separate login.
3. **It's not an IT project.** No code. Live in under 3 hours with minimal IT support.

## Proof points to use

Everything below is approved for customer-facing copy unless it's marked internal. Use the wording as written.

### Setup and sync

- **Setup time:** "Live in under 3 hours with minimal IT support." Since general availability on May 15, 2026, 41 customers have connected SyncBridge. Average setup is 2 hours 25 minutes.
- **IT involvement:** median 25 minutes, usually to confirm permissions or credentials.
- **Platforms connected so far:** SAP S/4HANA 16, Oracle NetSuite 14, Microsoft Dynamics 365 11.
- **Sync window:** updates appear within 15–20 minutes. It is not real-time.
- **Source of truth:** the ERP owns the vendor roster by default, and compliance status always writes back. Admins can optionally allow two-way updates for non-financial fields (contact name, email, vendor category).
- **Setup help:** a Dynamics 365 permission checklist and a custom field-mapping guide are available.
- **Compliance-gated POs (optional):** pilot them on the highest-risk vendors first.

### Beta results (5 customers, March 9 to May 14, 2026)

**Time to live:**

- **Cascadia Foods (SAP S/4HANA):** 1 hr 15 min, self-serve from the docs.
- **Midwest Component Manufacturing (Oracle NetSuite):** under 90 minutes.
- **BlueLine Transport (Oracle NetSuite):** 1 hr 40 min, self-serve.
- **Vertex Industrial (SAP S/4HANA):** 1 hr 50 min, including a 20-minute IT call on permissions.
- **Ironclad Group (Microsoft Dynamics 365):** 2 hr 15 min, with about 20 minutes of IT time for permission scopes.

**Before and after:**

- **BlueLine Transport:** three carriers missed the manual import in four months. Now the roster gap is closed, and finance sees compliance status in NetSuite before approving payment.
- **Ironclad Group:** 12–15 subs a year slipped through the manual import. Now new subs sync automatically, and compliance-gated POs are fully active.
- **Vertex Industrial:** IT had blocked a second vendor system that wasn't tied to SAP. Now SAP is the source of truth, status writes back, and gated POs are piloted on the highest-risk categories.
- **Midwest Component Manufacturing:** occasionally missed adding a vendor. Now that gap is "structurally impossible," and gated POs are piloted on about 40 of 198 vendors.
- **Cascadia Foods:** passed in late 2024 because their VP of Finance required SAP visibility. They re-engaged within a week of the announcement and signed within two, without a demo.

**What the beta customers said:**

- All 5 described the before-state as "two systems telling two different stories."
- 4 of 5 named roster sync as a benefit they hadn't anticipated.
- 3 of 5 saw a cross-department benefit: finance or operations seeing compliance data they didn't have before.
- Compliance-gated POs: 2 of 5 active, 2 evaluating, 1 not yet considered.

- **Calling SyncBridge free or "at no cost."** It's on the Scale plan only. Professional customers upgrade, prorated.
- **"Real-time."** Say "updates appear within 15–20 minutes."
- **"No IT" or "no IT ticket."** Say "minimal IT support."
- **"Enterprise-grade,"** or phrasing like "scale on Scale." Say "the Scale plan."
- **Adoption figures** (60%, 75%) in any customer-facing copy.
- **The retention or "stickiness" argument** in any customer-facing copy.

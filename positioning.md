## The canonical statement

> **TraceDesk is the vendor compliance tracking tool that runs itself — built for procurement teams who've outgrown the spreadsheet.**

**Support line (use whenever there is room):**

> Your vendors upload and renew their own certificates. You manage the exceptions.

**Tagline:** Stop chasing vendors for expired certificates.

**Why this statement.** From a customer's point of view it does three things in one sentence. It names the value (the system runs itself, so the chasing stops), the uniqueness (vendors do the work, not procurement) and the buyer's own situation (they've outgrown the spreadsheet). "Runs itself" is figurative, so the support line follows it to set the right expectation: the system does the routine work, and a person handles the exceptions.

**Rules for "runs itself"**
- Always pair it with the support line, or with a concrete example of what runs (reminders, uploads, escalation).
- Avoid "fully automatic," "zero effort" and "never think about it."

## Who it's for

**Company:** mid-market companies with 100–1,000 employees (core 200–500) and 30–500 vendors in manufacturing, logistics, food & beverage, construction and facilities management, managing vendor documents in spreadsheets, email and shared drives. 0 or 1 dedicated compliance coordinator. See `icp.md`.

| | Persona | What they care about |
|---|---|---|
| **Buys** | Procurement Manager (champion and budget owner); COO, VP Operations or Director of Procurement (economic buyer); IT lead (technical evaluator on ERP deals) | Time saved (champion); risk avoided and ROI (economic buyer); no new silo (IT) |
| **Uses** | Procurement Manager or Coordinator; Food Safety & Compliance Manager; executives viewing RiskMap; finance viewing status in the ERP; vendor contacts uploading documents | Handling exceptions, audit prep, seeing risk early, one source of truth |

In food & beverage the daily user is often the Food Safety & Compliance Manager. TraceDesk is still positioned as a vendor-compliance tool for the team that does the vendor paperwork, not as an internal audit platform.

## What we do

TraceDesk gives vendors a self-service portal to upload and renew their own compliance documents. It sends reminders at 90, 60 and 30 days before expiry, tracks expiry live, escalates automatically when a vendor ignores reminders, and scores every vendor so the riskiest surface first. On Scale, SyncBridge keeps the vendor roster in sync with the ERP and writes compliance status back to ERP vendor records.

| Module | Plain-language outcome | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| CertVault | One place for every certificate, with live status | All |
| RenewalPulse | Reminders go out without anyone pressing send | Reminders on all; escalation and configurable sequences on Professional and up |
| RiskMap | Know who is at risk before the audit | Professional and up |
| SyncBridge | Your vendor roster and your ERP tell the same story | Scale |

## The mechanism

Vendors manage their own compliance. In the spreadsheet world, procurement sits in the middle of every renewal. CompliGrid organizes documents but doesn't take procurement out of the loop. TraceDesk does. Customers describe the value in terms of what vendors now do, not what the software does, so lead with the vendor outcome.

## Vertical positioning

The core positioning holds in every vertical. What changes is the trigger, the regulatory language, the cost of a gap and the proof story. Use the local term for vendors first (see `icp.md`).

### Food & beverage (priority vertical, NPS +61)

> TraceDesk tracks every supplier's food safety certifications automatically — so you're never caught short at an audit.

- **Trigger language:** "Our inspection is coming up and I'm not confident we have everything"; "We had a supplier whose SQF cert lapsed and we didn't catch it"; "Annual certification renewals are eating my entire Q1."
- **Regulatory terms:** FDA, USDA, CFIA (Canada), SQF, BRCGS, FSSC 22000, HACCP.
- **Proof:** Suncrest Valley Foods (zero gaps at FDA audit; ready in 45 minutes; 14 hours of prep cut to under 90 minutes; 3 suppliers flagged before SQF lapse). Harvest & Grain Co. (about 40 Q4 renewals completed unaided).
- **Objection:** "We already have a process." So did Suncrest: a spreadsheet one person maintained, and two weeks of pulling documentation every audit season.
- **Headline:** Stop scrambling before audits. TraceDesk tracks every supplier's food safety certifications automatically.

### Construction

> TraceDesk ensures every subcontractor's insurance and safety certificates are current before work begins, and stays on top of renewals so you're never the one who missed it.

- **Trigger language:** "A sub's COI lapsed mid-project and we had to stop work"; "We're adding subs faster than the spreadsheet can keep up."
- **Documents:** COI, general liability, workers' comp (WSIB clearance in Ontario), safety training, OSHA compliance or provincial equivalents, COR, bonding.
- **Proof:** Halloran Construction (zero delays from lapsed certificates in 16 months; last lapse cost about $140,000; Professional plan about $9,588 a year).
- **Objection:** "We already check certs before we onboard a sub." The problem isn't onboarding. It's renewals six months into a project when no one is watching.
- **Headline:** One lapsed certificate cost $140,000. TraceDesk costs under $10,000 a year.

### Manufacturing

> TraceDesk tracks every supplier's quality and safety certifications automatically — so your next audit doesn't turn into a two-week documentation scramble.

- **Documents:** ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, IATF 16949, supplier qualification, safety certifications, insurance.
- **Proof:** Redvale Components (11 hrs/week saved; 100% audit compliance; live in 4 days; $0 on consultants).
- **Objection:** "Our ERP handles supplier records." Your ERP stores supplier data; it doesn't track certificate expiry, send renewal reminders or give suppliers a portal. With SyncBridge, compliance status writes back to your ERP vendor records.
- **Headline:** 187 suppliers. 11 hours saved every week. 100% audit compliance.

### Logistics

> TraceDesk keeps every carrier's compliance documentation current — automatically — so your team can manage hundreds of carriers without adding headcount.

- **Documents:** Operating authority (FMCSA in the US; NSC number or Ontario CVOR in Canada), certificates of insurance, cargo insurance, workers' comp, safety ratings.
- **Proof:** PacFirst Freight (about $68,000 per year hire avoided; 94% portal adoption in 30 days; onboarding 9 days to 2).
- **Objection:** "Carriers won't bother with a new portal." 94% of vendors are on the portal within 30 days across our customers; uploading once is easier than emailing certificates to every customer.
- **Headline:** 312 carriers. Same team. No new hire.
